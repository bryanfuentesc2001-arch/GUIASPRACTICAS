DBM Studio

DBM Studio is a private, personal desktop tool used only by its owner, Bryan Fuentes, to produce videos for his own YouTube channels (Selah Epics and Planet Before) and to upload them and reply to their comments.

It is not offered to the public, it has no other users and it does not collect data from anyone. It runs only on the owner's computer.

What it does with YouTube (YouTube Data API v3): uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's channels with their title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions, schedules them, reads new comments on those channels and posts the replies the owner approves.
